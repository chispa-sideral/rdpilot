<objective>
Relocate the daemon<->client transport primitives — socket-path resolution, length-prefixed JSON framing, and the `connect_or_spawn` auto-start helper — out of `rdpilot-daemon` (which pulls the full IronRDP/rustls/tokio-full stack via `rdpilot`) and into `rdpilot-ipc` (the dependency-free crate both the daemon and the future CLI already link). This is the prerequisite that lets `rdpilot-cli` (Plan 13-05) auto-start and reach the daemon WITHOUT ever depending on `rdpilot` or `rdpilot-daemon`, satisfying the thin-client invariant behind CLI-01's "transparently auto-starting it on first use".

Purpose: A CLI that reused the daemon crate's `connect_or_spawn`/`socket_path` would transitively pull in IronRDP — violating the locked thin-client rule (CONTEXT.md; research Summary finding 2 / Pitfall 4). The only correct fix is one shared, `rdpilot`-independent implementation, not two synchronized copies (research "Don't Hand-Roll": byte-identical framing/socket-path is a hard correctness requirement between the two processes).
Output: `rdpilot-ipc::transport` module owning the moved primitives; `rdpilot-daemon` rewired to consume them; `rdpilot-ipc` verified free of `rdpilot`/IronRDP.
</objective>
