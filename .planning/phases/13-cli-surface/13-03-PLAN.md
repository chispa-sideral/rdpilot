<objective>
Close the first of Phase 12's two pre-existing architecture gaps: the daemon's `ManagedSession` trait exposes only `close()`/`describe()`, with no seam to call `screenshot`/`send_mouse`/`upload_file`/etc. on the live session the registry holds. Extend the trait with `&self`-based async operational methods, change the registry's per-session storage from `Box<dyn ManagedSession>` to `Arc<tokio::sync::Mutex<Option<Box<dyn ManagedSession>>>>`, and add a `Registry::call` helper that dispatches an operation without holding the synchronous outer registry lock across `.await`.

Purpose: Every operational verb (CLI-02 perception/input/launch, CLI-03 file) is dispatched through this seam (Plan 13-04). The `Arc<TokioMutex<Option<Box>>>` shape is mandatory because the "obvious" `Arc<dyn ManagedSession>` + `Arc::into_inner` design does NOT compile (you cannot move an unsized `dyn Trait` out of an `Arc` — research Pitfall 2), and holding the outer `std::sync::Mutex` across `.await` deadlocks the single-`LocalSet`-OS-thread daemon (research Pitfall 3). The per-session serialization this yields is also exactly Phase 14's MCP-06 non-blocking-isolation property, built early.
Output: Extended `ManagedSession` + `impl for Session`; new registry storage + `Registry::call`; all test fakes updated with canned-value operational stubs so the offline CLI-02 integration proof (Plan 13-06) can render real tables against the fake connector.
</objective>
