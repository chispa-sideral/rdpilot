<objective>
Extend the Phase-11 `rdpilot-ipc` wire protocol with the three session-lifecycle verbs the daemon needs — `Request::Connect`, `Request::List`, `Request::Disconnect` — plus a `WireResponse::Connected` reply, a `WireErrorCode::DuplicateSession` collision code, and a `SessionLifecycle::Orphaned` status. This is the Wave-0 prerequisite the research flags: nothing else in Phase 12 can be end-to-end typed or tested until these verbs exist.

Purpose: SESSION-01 (open under a name / auto-id), SESSION-03 (`list`), and SESSION-04 (disconnect + uniqueness) all require wire verbs that Phase 11 deliberately left out of its boundary. D-29/D-30 already delegate ownership of this crate's session-identity and status-vocabulary types to Phase 12; these verbs are the same category of extension on the `Request`/`WireResponse` side. `WireErrorCode` is `#[non_exhaustive]` precisely to allow this.

Output: An extended, dependency-free (`rdpilot`-free) `rdpilot-ipc` crate whose new verbs preserve Phase 11's SESSION-02 required-`session` guarantee for every verb that operates on an existing session, while accurately modeling that Connect/List target no existing session.
</objective>
