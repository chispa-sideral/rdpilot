The error map is the only place that legitimately depends on both `rdpilot` and `rdpilot-ipc` (D-28: mapping deferred out of Phase 11 to here).

Output: A compiling `rdpilot-daemon` crate with all modules declared (stubs where later-filled), the seams and error map real and unit-tested, and `main.rs` calling `run()`.
