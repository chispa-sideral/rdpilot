<objective>
Make `rdpilot` dogfoodable on this box: author a version-controlled `SKILL.md` documenting the CLI (D-1/D-3/D-5) and a repeatable, idempotent installer that builds+installs both binaries, seeds a placeholder-only config, installs the skill to both agent-discovery surfaces, and self-verifies via the D-8 smoke test.

- Safety/session-naming wording (D-4/D-6): framed as "always disconnect explicitly" (correct, not merely defensive — idle-reap is 30 min per RESEARCH) and a self-owned `<agent>-<purpose>` session-name convention with a pre-connect `rdpilot list` collision check.
